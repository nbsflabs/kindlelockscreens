Make your Kindle lock screen

Adjust

Free, with no sign-up. You get a Kindle file and the image, made right here on your device.

On a Kindle it shows as the cover of the book you opened last, so it needs Display Cover and a Kindle without lock screen ads. What happens when I read another book? Using a Kobo or KOReader? See the setup guides.

How to get it on your Kindle
  1. Tap Download.
  2. Send the file to your Kindle: from a phone or tablet, tap Send to Kindle app; on a computer, download it and use Amazon's Send to Kindle.
  3. Open it on your Kindle once so it becomes the current book.
  4. Turn on Display Cover in Settings (under Device Options, or Screen and Brightness on newer models).

Display Cover is only available on Kindles without Special Offers (lock screen ads).

The Kindle app couldn’t take the file. Tap Download for Kindle instead, then send it with Amazon’s Send to Kindle.

  1. Tap Send to Kindle app and pick the Kindle app from the list.
  2. Download the file for Kindle, then send it with Amazon’s Send to Kindle, on the web or by email. Copying it over a USB cable won’t work: Amazon has to convert it.
  3. When it appears in your Kindle library, open it once.
  4. Turn on Display Cover in your Kindle’s settings.
  5. Opening another book swaps the lock screen for that book’s cover. Open this one again for a moment to bring it back.
  1. Connect your Kobo to a computer with a USB cable.
  2. Open the Kobo drive and show hidden folders. Inside the .kobo folder, make a folder called screensaver (if it isn’t there already).
  3. Copy the image into .kobo/screensaver, then eject the Kobo.
  4. On the Kobo, open Settings → Energy saving and privacy and turn on both Show current read and Show book covers full screen. Your image appears next time it sleeps.
  1. Copy the image onto your reader, for example into a screensavers folder.
  2. In KOReader, open the menu, then Settings → Screen → Sleep screen → Wallpaper, and choose Show custom image (or random image from folder) and pick it.
  3. On a jailbroken Kindle using a screensaver hack instead, copy it into that hack’s screensavers folder.
