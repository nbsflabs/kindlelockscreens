Setup guides

Kindle (no jailbreak)

Kindles have no setting for your own lock screen picture, but they can show the cover of the book you opened last. The maker gives you a tiny one-page book whose cover is your lock screen.

You need: a Kindle e-reader with the Display Cover setting, and no lock screen ads (Amazon calls them Special Offers). More about ads.

  1. In the maker, choose your Kindle, make your lock screen and tap Download.
  2. On a phone or tablet: tap Send to Kindle app and pick the Kindle app from the share list.
    On a computer: tap Download for Kindle (the file ends in .epub) and send it with Amazon’s Send to Kindle, on the web or by email. Copying it over USB won’t work: Amazon has to convert it.
  3. Wait for it to appear in your Kindle library (usually a minute or two, with Wi-Fi on), then open it once.
  4. Open Settings. Turn on Display Cover: it is under Device Options on older models, or Screen and Brightness on newer ones.
  5. Press the power button. Your lock screen appears.

Reading something else? Display Cover shows whichever book you opened last. Open the lock screen book again for a moment before you put the Kindle to sleep to bring it back.

Kobo

Recent Kobo software can show your own pictures when the reader sleeps. If you put several in the folder, it picks a different one each time, so the Download every design (ZIP) button in the maker works well here.

  1. In the maker, choose your Kobo model and tap Download for one picture, or Download every design (ZIP) and unzip it.
  2. Connect the Kobo to a computer with a USB cable and tap Connect on the Kobo.
  3. Open the Kobo drive. Turn on hidden files (Mac: press Cmd + Shift + . in Finder; Windows: View → Show → Hidden items in File Explorer).
  4. Open the .kobo folder and create a folder inside it called screensaver, if there isn’t one already.
  5. Copy your picture or pictures into .kobo/screensaver, then eject the Kobo safely.
  6. On the Kobo, open Settings → Energy saving and privacy and turn on both Show current read and Show book covers full screen. Your picture is shown on top of the sleep screen.

Menu names can differ slightly between Kobo software versions. If you don’t see the option, update your Kobo from Settings → Device information first.

KOReader and jailbroken Kindles

KOReader is a free, open-source reading app that runs on jailbroken Kindles, Kobos and other e-readers. It has its own sleep screen setting.

  1. In the maker, choose “KOReader or a jailbroken Kindle” with your screen size, then download one picture or the ZIP of every design.
  2. Copy the picture or pictures onto your reader over USB, for example into a folder called screensavers.
  3. In KOReader, open the top menu, then Settings (the cog) → Screen → Sleep screen → Wallpaper.
  4. Choose Show custom image or cover, then either pick your image, or choose a folder to show a random picture from it each time.

On a jailbroken Kindle using a screensaver hack rather than KOReader, copy the pictures into the folder that hack uses. Jailbreaking is done at your own risk and is not something this site can help with.
