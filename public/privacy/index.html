Privacy policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

The short version

  • Your photos never leave your device. Lock screens and Kindle files are made in your browser, and nothing you make is sent to us.
  • There are no accounts, no payments, no adverts, no analytics and no tracking.
  • The site is open source, so anyone can check the code does what this page says.
  • The only information we have is the ordinary record our servers keep of each visit.

Who we are

Kindle Lock Screens is run by nbsf labs. We are the data controller for the information described here. You can contact us at hello@kindlelockscreens.com.

Your photos and designs

The lock screen maker runs in your browser. The photos you choose are opened and edited on your own device, and the finished image and Kindle file are made there too. We never receive them, so we cannot see, keep or share them.

Information we do collect

Like most websites, our servers record each request they receive: the IP address, the page requested, the time, and the browser type. We use these records only to keep the site secure and working, and keep them for up to 30 days.

The counters at the bottom of each page are two plain totals: visits and lock screens made. Your browser remembers, only until you close the tab, that it has already been counted (this is not a cookie). Nothing about you is added to the totals; to stop one person running the numbers up, the counter briefly holds IP addresses in memory and forgets them within an hour.

If you write to us, we keep the conversation for as long as it takes to help you, and for a reasonable period afterwards.

Cookies and tracking

We do not use cookies, analytics or tracking of any kind. The maker remembers which e-reader you picked in your own browser’s storage so you don’t have to choose it again; that never leaves your device. Fonts and scripts are served from our own server, so your browser does not contact third parties when you use the site.

Why we are allowed to use this information

Keeping the site secure and answering your questions are our legitimate interests.

Who we share it with

Only the services that run the site: our hosting provider (OVH) and Cloudflare, which carries traffic to our server. We do not sell personal information.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, or object to how we use it. Write to us at the address above. If you are unhappy with our answer, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ico.org.uk).

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the date at the top.
