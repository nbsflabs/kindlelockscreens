How it works

  1. 1

    Design it

    Upload a photo and frame it how you like, or pick a design and add your name. You see exactly what your Kindle will show.

  2. 2

    Download it

    It’s free, with no sign-up. You get a small file made for Kindle: a tiny “book” whose cover is your lock screen.

  3. 3

    Send it to your Kindle

    Use Amazon’s Send to Kindle, open the file once, and turn on Display Cover. Your lock screen appears the next time the Kindle sleeps.

Made with care for e-ink

  • Your original photo stays with you

    Everything happens on your own device. Your photo and your finished lock screen never leave it: we don't receive or store them.

  • Tuned for the screen

    Preview in grayscale, adjust brightness and contrast, and sharpen for e-ink so the result looks crisp.

  • iPhone photos welcome

    JPG, PNG, WebP and iPhone HEIC photos all work, on phone or computer.

  • Personal touches

    Add your name to a design, or put a line of text on your photo, like contact details in case your reader gets lost. Colour is kept for Kindle Colorsoft.

  • Sized for your reader

    Exact screen sizes for Kindle, Kindle Scribe, Kobo and KOReader, from 600 × 800 to 1860 × 2480.

  • Every design in one go

    Kobo and KOReader can show a different picture each time they sleep. Download every design as a ZIP, sized for your screen.

Common questions

Will it work on my Kindle?

It works on Kindle e-readers that have the Display Cover setting and do not show lock screen ads. Check your Kindle.

Are my photos uploaded anywhere?

No. Your lock screen is made right here in your browser, and your photo never leaves your device.

What happens when I read another book?

Display Cover shows the cover of the book you opened most recently. To bring your lock screen back, open the lock screen file again before putting your Kindle to sleep. More on this.
